Clinical NLP · LLM evaluation · Medical dialogue

I'm an MSc student in Natural Language Processing at MBZUAI and a member of HALO Lab, co-supervised by Dr. Xiuying Chen and Dr. Imran Razzak. I build benchmarks and methods for the parts of clinical work that exam-style datasets skip: multi-turn consultations, patients describing symptoms in their own words, noisy transcripts and handwritten prescriptions.

I'm currently a research intern at DFKI Kaiserslautern with Dr. Sheraz Ahmed, and before that I worked at Microsoft, Botit and Monta.ai
